The science of breathing.

呼吸的科學。

Controlled breathing directly activates the parasympathetic nervous system via the vagus nerve — the body's built-in calm switch. Each technique in iKima targets a specific physiological mechanism: CO₂ clearance, heart rate variability, cortisol reduction, or sleep onset.

有控制的呼吸透過迷走神經直接啟動副交感神經系統——這是身體內建的「冷靜開關」。iKima 的每種呼吸法都針對特定的生理機制：CO₂ 排除、心率變異性、皮質醇降低或睡眠啟動。

Heart rate variability (HRV) is the gold-standard marker of stress resilience. Regular breathing practice has been shown in peer-reviewed research to increase HRV, lower resting heart rate, and improve mood over weeks of consistent use.

心率變異性（HRV）是壓力韌性的黃金指標。同行評審研究顯示，定期呼吸練習可在數週內提升 HRV、降低靜止心率並改善情緒。

References: Zaccaro et al. 2018 (Front. Hum. Neurosci.) · Balban et al. 2023 (Cell Reports Medicine) · Cole et al. 1999 (NEJM)
